Baseline — Logo Concept Canvas

Four directions for the Baseline mark. The goal: feel precise and engineering-native, but cool enough that a developer wants this open in a tab.

Recommended

1. The Datum Line

A reference point on a single baseline.

A bold horizontal line with one node marking the reference. It is the literal visual meaning of the word "baseline" made into a symbol.

Strengths

  • Instantly reads as "reference point / source of truth"
  • Scales perfectly to favicon size
  • Can abstract into a "B" monogram later

Weaknesses

  • Risk of looking like a slider or audio EQ
  • Needs careful weight to avoid thinness

The baseline cuts through the field of work, suggesting perspective, clarity, and a foundation you view everything against.

  • Strong app-icon presence

Weaknesses

  • Less literal tie to project management

Recommendation: The Datum Line

It is the most balanced option. It literally visualizes the brand name, scales everywhere, and carries enough engineering precision without feeling cold. Save the waveform energy for loading states or activity indicators in the UI.

Typography Direction

Baseline

Use a geometric sans-serif with medium weight and tight spacing. Inter, Geist, or Söhne are safe. If you want a stronger engineering signal, consider a monospaced-adjacent sans like JetBrains Mono or Commit Mono for UI labels while keeping the wordmark clean.
